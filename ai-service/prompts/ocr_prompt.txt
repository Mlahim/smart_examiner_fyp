HANDWRITTEN CORRECTIONS / CROSSED-OUT TEXT
Students may cross out words using:
- a single line through the word
- multiple pen/pencil strokes
- scribbling over the word
- circling/scratching out the word
- overwriting the old word with a new word

If text is clearly crossed out, struck through, scribbled out, or intentionally cancelled:
- DO NOT include the cancelled text in the final `raw_text` or question `answer`.
- Transcribe the replacement/new text if it is clearly readable.
- If the student crossed out a word and wrote nothing in its place, omit the cancelled word.
- If it is unclear whether a mark is a correction or normal handwriting, preserve the text rather than deleting it.
- Never guess what a crossed-out word says and never treat it as valid student content.

IMPORTANT: Normal underlining, highlighting, circles used to emphasize valid text, and decorative marks must NOT be treated as deletion unless the text is clearly cancelled.
